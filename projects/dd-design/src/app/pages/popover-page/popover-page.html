Popover

A click-triggered overlay with a title and body content.

Bottom (default)

Click me Popovers are great for contextual help without leaving the page.

Top position

Open above Content appears above the trigger element.

Inputs

  • title: string
  • position: 'top' | 'bottom' | 'left' | 'right'

Slots

  • [popover-trigger] — the trigger element
  • Default content — popover body
